About Me

I'm a Technical Product Owner with over 12 years' experience in IT across financial services and regulated environments. For the last 6 years, I've worked in Technical Product Owner and Business Analyst roles at Mastercard and FBD Insurance, building on an earlier foundation in software testing at Fidelity Investments and TCS — combining a strong software testing foundation with hands-on product ownership. I'm now pursuing a Tech Product Manager role where technical credibility and product craft are genuinely valued.

I'm comfortable working with REST APIs, Postman, Swagger, and SQL, and I write clear, technically grounded user stories and acceptance criteria in close collaboration with engineering, QA, and architecture. I use AI tools like Microsoft Copilot and NotebookLM day-to-day for story drafting, acceptance criteria generation, and requirements synthesis, and I'm currently building further AI product skills through prompt engineering, RAG prototyping, and hands-on use of AI app-builder tools like Google AI Studio and n8n. I'm SAFe POPM certified, and passionate about consumer health and workflow automation as product domains — thriving in environments that offer autonomy, clear feedback, and room to grow.
