NutriMed Ask: AI Nutrition Chatbot

Overview

NutriMed Ask is a nutrition chatbot prototype, built in Google AI Studio, that answers user questions using content from the Irish Nutrition & Dietetic Institute (INDI). The goal was to prototype how grounded, source-based responses could be delivered through a clean conversational interface, using a low-code AI platform rather than a custom-engineered backend. The chatbot delivers nutrition guidance grounded in INDI content.

The Challenge

General-purpose LLMs can give plausible but unreliable nutrition advice. Users need answers grounded in trusted, authoritative content, not hallucinated recommendations. The challenge was to prototype a chatbot experience that surfaces relevant INDI content and returns answers users can trust, validating the product concept quickly using Google AI Studio before considering any custom backend build.

Approach

I scoped the prototype requirements, defined the user flows, and built the chatbot experience in Google AI Studio:

  • Structured INDI public content as grounding material for the chatbot
  • Designed conversational flows for common nutrition queries
  • Configured system instructions and grounding prompts to keep responses tied to INDI content and reduce hallucination
  • Tested responses against ambiguous and out-of-scope questions
  • Defined evaluation criteria around answer relevance, source attribution, and edge-case handling

Key Artefacts

  • Product requirements document (PRD) with user stories and acceptance criteria
  • Working chatbot prototype built in Google AI Studio
  • Structured INDI content set used as grounding material
  • Evaluation criteria for response quality, source grounding, and edge-case handling

Outcome / Impact

Delivered a working chatbot prototype that returns source-grounded nutrition answers using Google AI Studio. It handles multi-turn queries and demonstrates how a low-code AI platform can validate a product concept quickly, from requirements through prototype to evaluation.

What I Learned

Building this prototype taught me that grounding quality matters more than model choice: how source content is structured and how prompts are designed are product decisions, not just engineering details. My QA background proved valuable for defining edge cases: ambiguous queries, out-of-scope questions, and situations where the chatbot should say it doesn't know rather than guess.
