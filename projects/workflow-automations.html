Workflow Automations

Two pieces of workflow-automation work: one real Technical Product Owner engagement, one hands-on prototype

Process and workflow automation is a product domain I keep coming back to. Below are two examples: a Salesforce proof of concept I scoped as a Technical Product Owner to replace a manual onboarding process, and an n8n pipeline I built from scratch to get hands-on with AI-agent automation.

Vendor Onboarding Workflow

Investment Research Services · Technical Product Ownership

The Investment Research Services team managed relationships with external research vendors through a manual process built on spreadsheets and emails. There was no structured, trackable way to ensure due diligence steps were completed consistently, and no audit trail for a given vendor relationship. As Technical Product Owner, I gathered the requirements and scoped a Salesforce proof of concept to fix that.

  • Framed the need as a user story: vendors onboarded through a structured, tracked workflow, so due diligence is consistent and every relationship has a full audit trail
  • Gathered the onboarding requirements from the research team
  • Configured the POC in Salesforce: custom objects for vendor records, Lightning screens for data capture, process automation for step progression, and case management for workflow tracking

The POC replaced the spreadsheet-and-email process with a structured, tracked workflow, giving the team a consistent due diligence process and a full audit trail for every vendor relationship. It demonstrated scoping a product workflow from a real operational pain point through to a working proof of concept. Separately, I showed how I'd extend the use case today, with AI-driven screening while keeping a human in the loop for any flagged decision.

AI Agent Automation Pipeline (n8n)

Hands-on prototype

An n8n workflow where an AI agent generates a response to each incoming chat message, and the query and response are automatically logged to Google Sheets and sent by email. I built it end to end to get practical experience wiring an AI agent into a no-code automation tool, rather than just reading about how the pieces fit together.

  • A trigger fires on each incoming chat message
  • An AI agent node (Google Gemini chat model, with a web-search tool and simple memory) generates the response
  • A Google Sheets node appends each query and response as a new row
  • A Gmail node sends the query and response on by email

This is a proof of concept, not a measured production deployment. The same automation thinking shows up in my day-to-day work: I automated recurring document-spec updates with Microsoft Power Automate, cutting per-document update time from 20+ minutes to under 10, which added up given the updates had to be repeated across specs for multiple products.
